scenario: model summer camp, four turns at facility level 5

Found by asking what commands 601-605 are, after a captured career showed
Speed carrying two different trainings at the same displayed level:
"Volleyball Dive" outside Classic/Senior Jul-Aug and "Running" inside it.

The hypothesis this started from was that 601-605 were camp trainings
mislabelled as level 5, and that the extractor had been wrong since M0.
That was wrong, and the probe killed it cleanly: every camp command's
failure_rate is IDENTICAL to its facility's own level-5 row -- 601/536
against speed lv5 536, 602/523 stamina, 603/532 power, 604/548 guts,
605/324 wit. They are the level-5 trainings with their own presentation
(background, outfit, animation) and their own names in text_data
category 138 at 29-33, against 1-25 for the ordinary five-by-five grid.

So the VALUES were already extracted and already correct. What the
engine did not know is that the game hands you level 5 on those turns
whatever your facility is actually at.

The same probe settled a second open question: single_mode_training_effect
has no level column and exactly one row-set per command, so training
levels 2-4 genuinely are NOT in master.mdb. The M1 flag was right and
M2's solver is still the way to get them.

The turn window is not a guess. Concerts sit at 24/36/48/60/72, and the
capture puts "concert begins after this turn" on Junior Late Dec and
Classic Late Jun, fixing turn 1 at Junior Early Jan with two turns per
month. Camp is exactly the four turns after the 2nd and 4th concerts:
37-40 and 61-64. Junior Jul-Aug is not camp, which the capture confirms.

Camp trainings do NOT advance the facility's own use counter. One clean
counterexample settles it: Speed reached level 4 at frame 54 after uses
at 38, 47, 51 and 53, with camp Speed trainings at 44 and 45 sitting in
between. Had those counted, level 4 would have shown at frame 51, and
frame 51 reads Lvl 3. Flagged as observed rather than decoded.

MEASURED, and the honest version is that it is a wash:

  search, 5 seeds   2500 -> 2603 mean (+103, sd of the paired
                    difference 258, so not distinguishable at n=5)
  golden career     1748 -> 1704, slightly WORSE

The two halves of the mechanic nearly cancel. Eight turns of level-5
training is worth roughly what eight turns of foregone counter progress
costs, and for a policy that trains constantly the second half wins. The
golden file is re-pinned because this is a deliberate model change, and
its going down is the point rather than a regression.

The interesting consequence is strategic and not yet exploited: during
camp the marginal value of training a LOW-level facility is highest, and
the counter progress you forgo is progress you were not going to bank
anyway. Whether the search finds that on its own is untested.
