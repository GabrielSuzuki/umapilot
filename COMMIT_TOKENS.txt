scenario: a rainbow training pays twice, and friend outings pay tokens

Two income terms the engine did not have, both player-reported and one of
them visible in the 2026-09-05 capture all along.

A RAINBOW TRAINING PAYS TWICE

Not a bigger number in one currency: two separate currencies, each at the
full amount. The payout badges sit beside the performance-point column on
the training screen, and the capture shows them plainly:

  frame 23  Speed, rainbow      Da +18   Vo +18
  frame 26  Wit,   rainbow      Pa  +8   Co  +8
  frame 33  Wit,   rainbow      Vo +17   Co +17
  frame 49  Wit,   rainbow      Pa +13   Co +13
  frame 30  Wit,   no rainbow   Co +12            <- one badge

The amounts within a pair are always equal, so this is two rolls of the
same payout rather than one payout split. The second roll is an ordinary
one -- frame 26 landed on Wit's own primary and secondary, frame 23 paid
Speed's primary plus vocal, which is neither.

Measured, 3 seeds, real deck, untargeted plan():

  income   1137 -> 1499   (+32%, and now above the real career's >=1325)
  songs     8.3 -> 11.7   (+41%)
  stats    3143 -> 3111   (unchanged, within a +/-150 seed spread)

Income and songs move a long way; final stats do not. That is not a
contradiction -- the extra songs arrive late, and a song's value is its
per-training bonus compounded over the turns that remain. Bought at turn
60 it earns almost nothing. Getting songs EARLY is now the open problem,
where before the problem was getting them at all.

This is also what breaks a deck out of its own two currencies. A 3-Wit
2-Speed deck earns mental and dance and starves visual and vocal; the
second roll lands anywhere, and a shop that cannot pay for a song board
freezes -- measured at 59 turns out of 72, the longest run 16 turns.

FRIEND OUTINGS FIRE A TOKEN EVENT

Separate from the outing chain: the first click is guaranteed and later
ones fire at roughly 40-50%, paying 20 points of whichever currency you
have least of. Self-targeting, which is exactly the shape of the problem.

Modelled at 45% and 20 points, both named as player-reported. It makes
careers WORSE when a companion is declared (2820 against 3111), and that
is worth stating rather than hiding: the outing CHAIN's own rewards are
still unknown -- the extractor says so, `rewardsKnown: false, per-step
rewards live in the story assets` -- so the search sees most of an
outing's cost and only this fraction of its payoff, and takes outings it
should not. The model being right and the search then misusing it is a
policy problem; leaving the model wrong to flatter the search would not
be. Default behaviour is unchanged, because no caller declares a
companion unless the user names one.

THE GOLDEN CAREER CANNOT EXPRESS ANY OF THIS

Its digest did not move, which means no rainbow fired in it: the golden
policy trains whichever stat is furthest from its cap, so it spreads
across five facilities and no card reaches bond 80. A fixture that cannot
reach the state under test is not a regression net for it. The new unit
tests carry that weight instead, each paired with a cold control so that
"rainbow pays twice" cannot be confused with "everything pays twice".

Neither term is in master.mdb. `tokenRollWeights` was already
community-sourced and these are the same class of fact -- named in the
assumptions every projection carries, not buried in the dataset.
